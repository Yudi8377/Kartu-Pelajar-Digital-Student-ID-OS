# Roadmap

1. **Foundation** — PWA shell, schema, CI, scenario catalogue, secret-handling rules.
2. **Identity and tenant authorization** — choose an auth provider, sessions, membership checks, role policies, negative tests.
3. **School and student workflows** — validated import, duplicate detection, status lifecycle, auditable changes.
4. **Card issuance and QR verification** — secure random tokens, hash-only storage, expiry/revocation checks, rate limits.
5. **Operational readiness** — backup/restore exercises, monitoring, incident playbooks, accessibility, offline behavior.
6. **Pilot** — synthetic-data test, privacy/security review, limited school pilot with explicit approval.
7. **Scale and AI assistance** — AI agents may draft issues/PRs and tests; least privilege, sandboxed work, human approval for production.

No phase is considered complete until tests and review evidence are recorded.
